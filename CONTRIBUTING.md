# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in PRISM.

PRISM is offered under a noncommercial license, with commercial licenses sold
separately. To keep that possible, every contribution must be licensed to the
copyright holder with the right to relicense it, including commercially.

Before your first pull request can be merged, you will be asked to agree to a
Contributor License Agreement (CLA). Pull requests without a signed CLA
will not be merged.

By opening a pull request you confirm that the contribution is your own work
and that you have the right to submit it.
